# NIM Relay demo: two-phone screenplay

About 2 minutes in the final cut. You record both phones' screens. The editor puts them side by side and cuts the waits.

- Phone A is the sender. It holds the baton, races the leg and pays 1 NIM.
- Phone B is the receiver. It gets the baton and the 1 NIM.

## Part 1: get ready (not recorded)

1. On both phones open Nimiq Pay and check the green MAINNET tag at the top.
2. On both phones open https://nimrelay.xyz and make sure you're signed in. If you see Sign in at the top right, tap it and approve in Nimiq Pay.
3. On Phone B, tap Profile at the bottom right. Write down the runner name at the top. That's who Phone A will pass to.
4. On Phone A, check the Nimiq Pay balance shows at least 1 NIM plus a little extra for the fee.
5. On Phone A, go to World. You should see a gold "Your turn" banner with Carry and your baton's name. If you don't, tap Inbox. Under "Needs you now" there should be a card with a Carry button. Don't tap it yet.
6. If a "Want a 60-second tour?" card appears on either phone, tap I'll explore.
7. On both phones turn on Do Not Disturb, set brightness to full and close every other app.
8. Go back to the World tab on both phones.

## Part 2: start recording

1. On Phone B, swipe down from the top right corner and tap the screen record button. Wait for the 3-second countdown.
2. Do the same on Phone A.
3. Leave both phones untouched on World for 3 seconds. The editor uses the clocks in the status bars to line the two videos up.

## Part 3: the screenplay

Each scene gives what to do on each phone, and the caption the editor puts on screen.

### Scene 1, the world (final cut 0:00 to 0:10)

Phone A:
1. Don't touch anything for 5 seconds. Let the globe turn.
2. Drag the globe slowly with one finger so it spins a little.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: How far can one NIM travel?

### Scene 2, the Atlas (0:10 to 0:20)

Phone A:
1. Tap one of the glowing gold lines (route arcs) on the globe.
2. A sheet opens showing the route's name, verified runs and records. Hold still for 3 seconds.
3. Close it with the back arrow or by swiping down.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: The globe is the Relay Atlas. Every route is a real race track.

### Scene 3, carry the baton (0:20 to 0:28)

Phone A:
1. On the World screen, tap Carry on the gold "Your turn" banner. If it isn't there, tap Inbox at the bottom, then Carry on the card under "Needs you now".
2. On the relay's journey screen, tap Carry this leg.
3. The race loads. An arrival title shows, then a countdown. Don't touch the screen until it says GO.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: The baton reaches you. Now you race.

### Scene 4, the race (0:28 to 0:50)

Phone A, race the whole leg (about a minute). Controls:

- Swipe left or right to change lanes.
- Swipe up to jump over barriers.
- Swipe down to slide under beams.
- Stay on the gold ghost line to build FLOW.
- If a ghost runner is ahead of you, try to pass it.

If "Skip gameplay tutorial" shows at the top right, you can tap it, or leave the prompts in since they explain the controls on camera.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: Beat the ghost of the last runner. The server replays every move, so times can't be faked.

### Scene 5, leg complete (0:50 to 0:56)

Phone A:
1. You cross the finish and LEG COMPLETE shows with your time.
2. Wait 2 seconds so the time is readable.
3. Tap the gold PASS button (it says PASS plus your baton's name).

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: Verified finish.

### Scene 6, choose the route (0:56 to 1:02)

Phone A:
1. "WHERE DOES IT GO NEXT?" shows with route choices.
2. Tap one of the routes, such as the one marked Relay pick.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: You choose where the baton goes next.

### Scene 7, choose the runner (1:02 to 1:08)

Phone A:
1. "WHO CARRIES IT NEXT?" shows.
2. Tap Phone B's runner card, the name you wrote down in Part 1. If it isn't listed, tap Find a runner by handle and type that name.
3. A note step appears. Tap Pass without a note, or type a short note (like "go fast") and tap Attach note.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: And who carries it.

### Scene 8, the throw and the payment (1:08 to 1:20)

This is the most important scene. Go slowly.

Phone A:
1. The launch pad appears: "Hold the baton. Release to throw."
2. Press and hold the baton, drag back a little, then let go. The baton throws.
3. Nimiq Pay opens a payment screen: send 1 NIM to Phone B's wallet. Hold still for 2 seconds so it's readable.
4. Approve it (Face ID or passcode).

Phone B: nothing yet.

Caption: One real Nimiq transaction. 1 NIM, wallet to wallet.

### Scene 9, confirmation on both phones (1:20 to 1:35)

Phone A:
1. You're back in NIM Relay while the pass is checked on chain. Don't tap anything.
2. When it confirms, "Verified on Nimiq" shows and the app flies to World, with a banner reading "<Phone B's name> HAS THE BATON" and "Handoff #... complete".
3. Hold on that banner for 3 seconds.

Phone B, as soon as Phone A shows Verified on Nimiq:
1. Tap Inbox. Under "Needs you now" a card shows the baton is yours, with a Carry button. Hold 3 seconds.
2. Switch to Nimiq Pay and open the wallet. The incoming 1 NIM shows at the top of the activity. Hold 3 seconds.
3. Switch back to NIM Relay.

Caption: Checked on chain. The baton changes hands.

### Scene 10, the journey (1:35 to 1:43)

Phone B:
1. Tap World at the bottom left, then View journey on the relay card. Don't tap Carry here, since that starts Phone B's race.
2. On the journey screen, scroll down slowly to the Atlas section. Your pass shows as the latest leg.

Phone A: nothing.

Caption: Every leg becomes part of the baton's journey.

### Scene 11, what keeps people coming back (1:43 to 1:52)

Phone A:
1. Tap Profile at the bottom right.
2. Scroll slowly past Relay Grants (it reads x / 5 NIM claimed) and the Atlas missions.
3. Tap Crew at the bottom for 2 seconds.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: New runners get their first baton free. Crews, rivals and missions keep the relay moving.

### Scene 12, the proof (1:52 to 2:00)

Phone A:
1. Open https://nimrelay.xyz/proof. Type it in the address bar, or find Proof in the app.
2. Scroll to the handoff you just made. Hold 3 seconds on the transaction line.

Phone B: nothing.

Caption: Every pass is on chain. Catch it. Carry it. Pass it on.

## Part 4: stop and send

1. Stop screen recording on both phones (tap the red pill at the top, then Stop).
2. Recordings are saved to Photos on each phone.
3. Send both videos to the editor.
4. Send Phone A's transaction hash to the developer. In Nimiq Pay, open the 1 NIM transaction and copy its hash.

## Notes for the editor

- Sync the two recordings using the clocks in the status bars.
- Layout: Phone A full screen for scenes 1 to 8. Split screen for scene 9, Phone A's confirmation next to Phone B's inbox and wallet. Then Phone B for scene 10 and Phone A for 11 and 12.
- Cut the waiting. Confirmation takes 10 to 30 seconds. Keep only the moment it lands.
- Race: keep the best 15 to 20 seconds, ideally a lane change, a jump and the ghost together on screen.
- Blur the sender's wallet balance on the Nimiq Pay approval screen if asked.
- Captions: one short line each, bottom of the frame, clear of the phone's own text.
- End card: NIM Relay · https://nimrelay.xyz

## If something goes wrong

- If Nimiq Pay's approval is cancelled or fails, the baton stays with Phone A. Tap the throw again for another take. No NIM is lost.
- If confirmation seems stuck, don't close the app. It keeps checking the chain on its own.
- If the race ends in LEG FAILED, tap to try again. Only a finished leg can be passed.
